Know the basics

What is the difference in how a crime is charged?

A felony is the most serious classification of crimes and may carry significant penalties. The specific punishment depends on the offense and circumstances.
A misdemeanor is generally less serious than a felony. Some offenses can be reduced depending on the facts and applicable California law.
An infraction is generally punishable by a fine rather than jail time, although failing to appear can create additional consequences.
