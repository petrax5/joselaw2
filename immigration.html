Understanding permanent residence

Lawful permanent resident status is commonly referred to as getting a “green card.” It can be achieved through petitions and applications or through relief from removal granted by an immigration judge.
Permanent residency is a lawful status in the United States that allows a person to live, work, and travel freely, subject to the rules and limitations of that status.
One common path is through a family member with adjustment of status or consular processing. The U.S. government must approve the application or other requested immigration relief.
