Our Story

A law office built around honest, experienced representation.

The Law Office of José Alfredo Hernández, A.P.C. offers vigorous legal representation in criminal and immigration proceedings.

Our story

Guidance when the stakes are high.

Mr. Hernández has built a reputation for helping individuals navigate some of the most difficult times of their lives through honest, forthcoming and experienced representation.

At the office, the initial consultation is directly with Mr. Hernández. From day one, he determines whether he can assist and, if so, what course of action may be appropriate. If he cannot assist, he takes the time to explain why and offer guidance on possible next steps.
